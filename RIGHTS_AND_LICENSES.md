# Rights and licence decision

The corresponding author requested preparation of data/code for public access and confirmed no institutional release restriction. The final data and software licences have not been selected or issued in this package. Suggested CC BY 4.0 for data and MIT for code in the historical deposit metadata are options, not executed grants.

Before public release, the rights holders should confirm redistribution rights and choose appropriate licences, retaining any third-party notices. Add the agreed licence texts and metadata to the public repository/release. Public visibility alone does not grant broad reuse rights. No coauthor consent is fabricated by these files.
