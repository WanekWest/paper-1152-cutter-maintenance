# ER-07B-R2F claim resolution

## Claim 1 — `CLAIM_1_PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`

- B7: supported; P-R4 is favourable, but the mean magnitude is below `Delta_star=0.03`.
- B4: not supported; B4 is decisively favourable. This comparator is reported without concealment.
- B1: supported; P-R4 is favourable and the mean magnitude reaches/exceeds `Delta_star=0.03`.

Eligible wording is restricted to B7 and B1 and must immediately disclose the unfavourable B4 result.

## Claim 2 — `CLAIM_2_SUPPORTED`

The information-action ablation changed 235/256 paired decisions (91.80%); the control used an information action in 235 episodes, the variant used none, and no control constraint breach or probability-over-epsilon event occurred. This supports an information-action decision-effect claim, not field effectiveness.

## Claim 3 — `CLAIM_3_PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`

Planned-replacement frequency rose from 0.360795 at S0 to 1.000000 at S1, then remained 1.000000 at S2. This supports a threshold/plateau response, not a strictly graded three-level response.

## Boundary

All records are `T6_SCIENTIFIC_LOCKED_EXPERIMENT`. The evidence is model-based and limited to one indexed cutter position. Whole-machine safety, mine safety, personnel safety and accident reduction are not claimed.
